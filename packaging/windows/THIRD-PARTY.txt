ngteams third-party components

The dependencies/ directory carries the Cargo dependency inventory and the
upstream license/notice files from the exact package sources used by the build,
plus libwebrtc's consolidated third-party notice. Build dependencies are included.

The adjacent GStreamer license directories and ../versions.txt record the shipped
media components. MEDIA-MANIFEST.json records their hashes, versions and source
recipe URL. These DLLs remain separately replaceable under their upstream terms.
The Microsoft VC redistributable license is under microsoft-vc-runtime/.

The Lucide icon, Fluent Emoji reaction and MediaPipe segmentation model notices
are included under dependencies/assets/. The model is compiled into the app.

Microsoft WebView2 is installed separately from Microsoft's Evergreen service,
under Microsoft's terms. Its installer is downloaded only when the runtime is
missing, and its Microsoft Authenticode signature is checked before execution.

Source for ngteams: https://github.com/nickalie/ngteams
The exact source commit is recorded in PACKAGE-MANIFEST.json.
